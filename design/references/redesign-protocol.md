# Redesign Protocol

Attribution: adapted from the MIT-licensed [Taste Skill](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md#taste-skill).
Scope: redesigning an existing website, landing page, or portfolio without erasing useful brand equity.

## First Action

Classify the redesign before changing code:

| Mode | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Preserve | Keep brand, content hierarchy, and interaction model. Improve execution. |
| Targeted evolution | Keep identity, modernize weak areas, improve clarity and conversion. |
| Full redesign | Rebuild structure, aesthetic, and interaction model with explicit approval. |

If the request does not say preserve or overhaul, default to targeted evolution.

## Audit Before Touching

- Inventory current logo, colors, type, imagery, nav, sections, CTAs, forms, and proof.
- Identify what works: distinctive assets, real screenshots, trust signals, domain-specific copy.
- Identify what fails: unclear hierarchy, stale spacing, weak contrast, broken mobile, generic stock, fake data.
- Capture desktop and mobile screenshots when a live surface exists.

## Preservation Rules

- Do not change brand colors, logo, product names, pricing claims, legal claims, customer logos, or testimonials silently.
- Do not replace real product screenshots with generic illustrations.
- Do not delete content that communicates trust, compliance, or core differentiation without moving it elsewhere.
- Preserve information architecture unless the redesign mode explicitly permits restructuring.

## Modernization Levers

Apply in this order:

1. Hierarchy: headline, CTA, proof, section order.
2. Spacing: rhythm, section padding, mobile margins.
3. Typography: scale, line height, measure, weight count.
4. Color: contrast and accent discipline.
5. Materiality: radius, borders, cards, shadows.
6. Motion: purposeful transitions only after static layout works.

## Decision Tree

- If users recognize the brand and the product proof is real, preserve identity and polish execution.
- If layout is dated but content is strong, keep content and rebuild composition.
- If copy is vague and visuals are fake, rewrite and rebuild from the design read.
- If the existing site has no usable assets, document the absence and choose a fresh system.
